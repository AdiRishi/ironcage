## Outcome

Compare current financial history and save queries for reuse.

Prerequisite: Stage 3 is verified. Reuse its interpretations and financial arithmetic.

## User flows and frontend

- Open an overview of spending, income, surplus, cash changes, and loan repayment effects with dates, accounts, and coverage visible.
- Compare periods and inspect category, merchant, account, tag, or personal-event contributors, purchase counts, and average payments.
- Open contributing records with the same query scope and inspect calculated contributions separately from bank amounts.
- Save a named query with fixed or relative dates. Reopen or refresh it to calculate from current records.
- Understand partial periods, missing coverage, zero baselines, and overlapping groups through explicit labels and accessible chart/table views.

The stage is complete only when these flows use real persisted records and work on desktop, mobile, and with a keyboard. Empty, loading, review, and failed states belong to the same implementation.

## Backend and integration

Implement current-data queries, comparison arithmetic, contribution queries, and saved definitions. Related totals use a consistent database read. The UI invalidates visible TanStack Query results after changes. A drill-down returns its current headline with its rows. Closed saved views require no computation.

Store query definitions, not a mandatory archive of every result. Relative dates resolve when viewed; fixed dates stay fixed. Extend export to saved definitions.

## Completion evidence

Compare independently known totals and contributors, including matching elapsed dates, zero prior values, missing accounts, overlapping tags, and purchases moving between debit and credit. Show a correction updating an open view and a saved query using new records on its next opening. Check fixed and relative dates across a calendar boundary. Contributions and their displayed headline must agree after intervening edits.

Preserve earlier stage regressions and report specific unverified external checks. Stop after this stage.

## Excluded

Ordinary costs, schedules, forecasts, automatic saved-query refresh, historical snapshot storage, analyst conversations, and findings.
