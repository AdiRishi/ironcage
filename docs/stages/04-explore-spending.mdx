title: "4. Explore historical spending"
description: Explore actual financial history with consistent comparisons, evidence, and saved results.
---

## Outcome

The user can compare historical periods, identify contributors, inspect the supporting transactions, and save a useful analysis. A correction updates current results while older answers retain their original basis.

Prerequisite: stage 3 is verified. Reuse its financial measures and interpretations.

## User flows and frontend

- Open an overview of actual spending, income, surplus, cash changes, and loan repayment effects with period, accounts, and coverage visible.
- Compare periods in Trends, drill into categories, merchants, accounts, tags, or personal events, and inspect purchase counts and average payments.
- Follow a displayed contribution to its evidence while preserving query scope and back-navigation state. Distinguish calculated contributions from booked amounts.
- Save fixed or relative analyses, choose automatic or manual refresh, and inspect current and historical results. Show pending refresh and preserve the last completed result.
- Understand partial periods, missing coverage, zero comparison bases, overlapping groups, and unavailable measures through explicit labels and accessible chart alternatives.

The home page becomes the historical overview. Upcoming commitments, findings, and analyst actions arrive in their later stages. Actual spending includes exceptional purchases; ordinary-cost estimates arrive in stage 5.

## Backend and integration

Add comparison queries, frequency decomposition, contribution queries, immutable result snapshots, saved definitions, analysis jobs, and revision-aware refresh. Reuse stage 3 arithmetic rather than creating calculations in React or chart components.

Capture inputs at one database snapshot. Preserve exact totals, coverage, date basis, and supporting credits outside the visible period. Imports and corrections refresh affected analyses. Time-driven refresh resolves relative dates without rewriting historical results. Extend export, removal invalidation, and restore checks to saved definitions and results.

## Completion evidence

Demonstrate a comparison through contributing transactions, a saved analysis refreshing after import and correction, and an older result retaining its calculation basis. Verify matching elapsed dates, zero baselines, missing-account coverage, overlapping tags, and migration of purchases between debit and credit accounts.

Use independently known counts and amounts for frequency and average-payment comparisons. Exercise fixed/manual and rolling/automatic saved definitions across date changes. Verify that export and restored results retain their evidence and definitions.

## Excluded

Ordinary costs, recurring schedules, commitments, forecasts, scenarios, analyst actions and conversations, proactive findings, and intentions. Correlation-based explanations and overlapping-service investigations belong to later analyst and insight work.
