## Outcome

The user receives evidence-backed findings and can follow a deliberate change against a chosen baseline. New imports and corrections update findings and progress without repeatedly announcing unchanged information.

Prerequisite: stage 6 is verified. Reuse its financial analysis, schedules, context, and investigation flows.

## User flows and frontend

- Inspect ranked findings, open their records, ask the analyst to investigate, add context, mark useful, or dismiss.
- Understand superseded or reopened findings through the specific new evidence that changed them.
- Turn a finding into a tracked intention with a measure, baseline, start date, and context. Edit, pause, complete, and review its progress, including explicit substitution costs.
- Use the in-app inbox for findings, completed work, failures, and questions. Follow each item to its existing owning view.
- Configure import reminders and review follow-up outcomes. Distinguish a request for newer data from a newly observed financial change.

Extend the overview with relevant findings and add complete Insights and intention experiences. Existing job failures and reviews remain accessible through their owning screens as well as the inbox.

## Backend and integration

Implement versioned insight detectors, materiality and ranking, grouping, dismissal/reopening rules, intentions, and inbox delivery. Refresh affected findings and intention results after imports, corrections, context changes, and scheduled reviews.

Preserve accepted user state. Coalesce repeated refresh and notifications. Time-driven work cannot invent new spending observations. Update exports, source/context invalidation, backup manifests, and restore verification for findings, intentions, and inbox state.

## Completion evidence

Demonstrate a material finding with supporting records, dismissal that survives unchanged refresh, reopening after qualifying new evidence, and supersession after a correction. Track reduced delivery spending with an explicitly selected grocery substitution measure and disclose insufficient evidence.

Verify inbox navigation, notification deduplication, import-reminder preferences, and scheduled work when no new data exists. Restore all new state without replaying completed notifications. Account for the complete spend-management requirements across the seven stage completion records.

## Excluded

Automatic bank feeds, payment execution, investment/trading/crypto systems, and external notification channels beyond the specified in-app behavior. Those need separate product decisions.
