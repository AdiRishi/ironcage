## Outcome

Add useful findings, intentions, and in-app reminders.

Prerequisite: Stage 6 is verified. Reuse existing financial queries and analyst entry points.

## User flows and frontend

- Inspect material findings with supporting periods and records, then mark useful, dismiss, or ask the analyst about them.
- Track a chosen measure against a fixed baseline and start date. Include related costs only when explicitly selected.
- Review progress, edit or pause an intention, and mark it complete.
- Use an in-app inbox that links to existing findings, reviews, or failures without duplicating their underlying state.
- Enable or disable import reminders and distinguish stale data from a new financial observation.

The stage is complete only when these flows use real persisted records and work on desktop, mobile, and with a keyboard. Empty, loading, review, and failed states belong to the same implementation.

## Backend and integration

Run bounded deterministic scans on opening the view or through an ordinary scheduled handler. Preserve finding identity and dismissed state across repeat scans. Recompute intention progress on demand or at a configured review. A failed scan offers retry and does not change financial history.

Use stable notification identity to avoid repeating unchanged information. Extend data export to findings and intentions. No generalized dependency-refresh coordinator or notification replay system is required.

## Completion evidence

Demonstrate a supported material finding, persistent dismissal, a genuinely new change period, and closure after a correcting edit. Track reduced delivery spending with an explicitly selected grocery comparison. Verify insufficient data, scan failure/retry, inbox navigation, reminder preferences, and repeated scans without duplicate notifications. Check cumulative earlier-stage regressions.

Preserve earlier stage regressions and report specific unverified external checks. Stop after this stage.

## Excluded

Automatic bank feeds, payment execution, investment/trading/crypto behavior, and external notifications.
