## Outcome

Manage commitments and compare transparent cost estimates and scenarios.

Prerequisite: Stage 4 is verified. Reuse current-data queries and saved definitions.

## User flows and frontend

- Review suggested recurring payments and confirm amount, cadence, account, effective dates, and payment matches.
- See upcoming scheduled cash movements, with card settlements and loan repayments distinct from costs.
- Inspect an ordinary-month estimate with its recent covered weeks, excluded events, and allocated commitments.
- View a 30- or 90-day cost outlook with its baseline, expected income, commitments, and explicit one-off assumptions.
- Save a scenario and compare changed costs or income, including any explicit substitution into another category.

The stage is complete only when these flows use real persisted records and work on desktop, mobile, and with a keyboard. Empty, loading, review, and failed states belong to the same implementation.

## Backend and integration

Implement confirmed schedules, effective changes, occurrence decisions, recent means, and deterministic scenario arithmetic. Remove already scheduled payments from the variable baseline before adding obligations once. Display historical balance dates and missing inputs accurately.

Calculate on open or explicit refresh. Forecasts estimate costs and surplus under stated assumptions; the cash calendar shows known scheduled movements. They do not simulate future cash/debt balances, card cycles, or interest. Extend export to schedules and scenario definitions.

## Completion evidence

Demonstrate a quarterly bill, an effective payment/account change, an occurrence decision, and ordinary-cost contributors adding to their headline. Compare a scenario against independently calculated baseline values. Verify no double counting of costs and settlements, clear baseline gaps, and preservation of actual records. Future estimates must be visibly distinct from historical balances and known payment dates.

Preserve earlier stage regressions and report specific unverified external checks. Stop after this stage.

## Excluded

Probabilistic forecasts, model tournaments, calibration/backtesting machinery, cash/debt simulation, analyst conversations, and findings.
