## Visual direction

The strongest graphic explains a change in spending. The surrounding controls preserve the selected period, accounts, measure, and evidence. Related records and the analyst sit beside the main view on desktop and open as separate views on mobile.

The proposed palette uses white working areas, a cool navigation background, and ink blue for actions and selection. Sea green and mulberry distinguish data series; labels and symbols carry their meaning alongside color.

Instrument Sans is the proposed type family. The images approximate it. Text is left aligned, monetary columns are right aligned, and financial values use tabular numerals. Control corners are tighter than the corners of larger panels.
