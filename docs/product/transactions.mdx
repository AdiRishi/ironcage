Stage 1 shows postings and their sources. Stage 2 adds statement pages. Stage 3 adds meaning: roles, categories, merchants, tags, links, and corrections.

## The list

Every posting from every account in one list, newest first, with filters for account, currency, dates, amount, description text, and open questions. From stage 3, filters also cover role, category, merchant, tag, and personal event, and search matches merchant names.

Each row shows the account, the date the bank booked it, the signed amount, and the description as the bank wrote it.

## The detail

The bank's fields stay at the top and never change: description, booked amount, posted date, value date if the bank gave one. Below them, every source that supports the posting: the file, the row or page, the literal text, and a link to the original file. A removed original is marked unavailable with a Reupload action.

From stage 3 the detail also shows the interpretation: role, category, merchant, tags, personal events, linked postings, credits applied, and the history of corrections.

## Money counted once

The spending view and the account view describe the same records differently. Examples are fictional.

| Records                                                     | Spending view             | Account view                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| $100 grocery purchase on the card, then a $100 card payment | $100 groceries            | Card debt up $100 then down $100; deposit account down $100.  |
| $2000 moved between two owned accounts                      | Nothing                   | Both movements visible.                                       |
| $300 dinner, $200 reimbursed by friends                     | $100 personal cost        | The payment and the two incoming amounts, on their own dates. |
| $120 purchase, $40 refund a month later                     | $80 in the purchase month | Both on their own dates.                                      |
| $3900 mortgage repayment, $2800 interest, same month        | $2800 interest            | $3900 out of the deposit account; loan down $1100 net.        |

A transfer is not automatically internal and an incoming amount is not automatically income. Rent paid by transfer is a cost. A loan drawdown is borrowing. When the other side of a movement is not in the records, the user can confirm what it was, and the detail shows that the counterpart is missing.

## Corrections

The user can change a role, category, merchant, tags, personal event, or purchase date, split a purchase into parts that add up, link a refund or reimbursement, link the two sides of a movement, or mark a fee as belonging to a purchase. A change that affects costs, income, cash, or debt shows its effect before it is accepted.

"Apply to this transaction" is the default. "Apply as a rule" previews every matching record and says which are exceptions that will keep their own setting. History lists every change with its scope and time. Undo adds a new change that restores the old meaning; nothing is erased.

## Dates

The account view uses the date the bank booked. Spending views use the purchase date when the app knows it and the booked date otherwise, and say which. A bank value date is shown but is not treated as the purchase date.

## Foreign purchases

The booked AUD amount is the amount. The original currency amount is shown beside it when the bank supplied one. An international fee is its own posting; the app can associate it with the purchase without merging the amounts.
