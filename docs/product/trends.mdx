Stage 4 delivers comparisons and saved analyses. Stage 5 adds the ordinary-cost view.

## Comparing periods

Pick a measure, a period, and what to compare it with: last month, the same month last year, a custom range, the last 90 days. Break the difference down by category, merchant, account, tag, or personal event. Every row shows its share of the change and links to its records.

Category, merchant, and account rows add up to the difference, with a remainder for the long tail. Tag and personal-event rows overlap and say so; they do not add up and the app does not pretend they do.

## Fair comparisons

A month in progress is compared with the same number of days of the other month, and is labelled partial. The user can switch to whole months or to a daily average. If an account's data stops partway through a period, the number says so; a drop caused by missing card data is not a saving.

Percentages appear beside amounts. When the earlier period was zero there is no percentage, just "new". Small samples are labelled small.

A higher average per purchase is reported as that, not as a price rise. Bank data does not say what was in the basket.

## Actual spending and an ordinary month

Actual spending includes everything, holidays and all. From stage 5, the ordinary-cost view estimates what a normal month costs: recent weeks averaged with named exceptions set aside, plus known commitments spread across the periods they cover. The user can see the weeks used, the events excluded, and the commitments included, and change the exclusions without touching actual spending.

## Saved analyses

Save the current question with a name. Opening it runs it again on current data. A fixed date range stays fixed. A relative one, such as the last three months, moves with the calendar.

## Questions this page answers

- Where did the money go this month?
- Which merchants explain the increase?
- More purchases, or dearer ones?
- What did the trip cost, including flights paid in March?
- Has an ordinary month got more expensive, setting the trip aside?
- Which commitments are new and which have stopped?
