## Rules that hold everywhere

- The same question over the same records gives the same number on every screen and in the analyst. Every number states its period, accounts, measure, basis, calculation time, and coverage.
- Uploading a file twice changes nothing. Corrections survive later uploads.
- A failed action can be retried from the screen it happened on. Retrying never duplicates a record.
- Missing data is shown as missing, never as zero.
- Any number can be opened to see the records behind it.

## Accounts in scope

Four CommBank accounts: a spending offset account, a savings offset account, a credit card, and a home loan. Mortgage repayments, strata levies, and the shift of everyday purchases from the deposit account to the card are part of normal analysis.

An account says where money sits, not what a payment was for. A bill paid from savings is still a cost.

## Out of scope

Automatic bank feeds, making payments, investments, trading, and crypto. Transfers toward an investment stay visible as movements.
