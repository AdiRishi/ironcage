Ironcage is a private web application for one person's bank accounts: deposit accounts, credit cards, and loans, starting with CommBank. These pages describe behavior from your side. The [design language](/design) describes how it looks.

## The period

Every screen except Settings reads one period. A strip of months runs along the top of the application, each month drawn as a short bar of its outflow, or of its spending on [Spending](/product/spending). Select a bar for that month, or the year under it for the whole year. Choose months, at the end of the strip, selects a run of whole months, such as March to May, and the strip inks every month in it. The choice changes every screen.

A month in progress runs to today in the timezone set in Settings. Without a chosen period, screens open on the current month, or on the latest month with records when this month has none yet. The period lives in the URL, so a bookmark reopens the same view against current data.

## Rules that hold everywhere

- The same question over the same records gives the same number on every screen and in the analyst.
- Any number opens to the records behind it.
- Missing data is shown as missing, never as zero. A month where the card statement is absent says so beside the number.
- An assignment you made wins over the model and over rules, and it survives later imports.
- A number that rests partly on the model's judgment says how much.
- Uploading a file twice changes nothing. A failed action can be retried where it failed, and retrying never duplicates anything.

## Out of scope for now

Automatic bank feeds, making payments, and other banks. Investments are on the [roadmap](/roadmap).
