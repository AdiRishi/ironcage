Ironcage is a private, responsive web app for Adi. It brings supplied financial records into one history, explains spending over time, and supports questions about future commitments and savings.

The scope includes every capability below. The sections organize the specification. They do not define a reduced release or a sequence of optional features.

## Product-wide behavior

The same financial question has the same numerical answer in a chart, a saved analysis, and an analyst response. Each answer identifies the period and records it uses.

Imports can be repeated safely. Corrections survive later uploads. A failure leaves an explanation and a way to continue, without damaging previously accepted history.

Adi can inspect the transactions behind a number, correct an interpretation, and see the affected views update. The app asks concrete questions when evidence is insufficient. It does not require an accounting questionnaire before import.

## Current financial context

The supplied history includes spending and savings accounts, a credit card, and a mortgage. Mortgage payments and strata levies affect ongoing commitments. Credit-card adoption is a known change in payment method.

Account labels describe where money sits. They do not determine the purpose of every transaction. An expense paid from a savings account remains an expense.

## Scope boundary

Recurring manual file import is the input path. The app ingests all supplied records, including older statements, and makes actual coverage visible.

This specification does not include executing payments, placing trades, or managing crypto positions. Those belong to later product definitions. Transfers toward an investment remain identifiable money movements in the spending history.
