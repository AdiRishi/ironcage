Everything here is stage 7.

## Findings

Insights lists changes worth a look: a subscription that went up, a fee that keeps appearing, a category that has been rising for months. Each finding says what changed, over which period, by how much, and links to the records or schedule behind it. Findings are ordered by the money involved and how long the change has lasted, so a 200% jump in a $2 item does not outrank a $50 rise in something monthly.

The user can mark a finding useful, dismiss it, add context, ask the analyst about it, or track it. A dismissed finding stays dismissed. A genuinely new change creates a new finding. A finding whose evidence disappears after a correction is closed, not left standing.

## Intentions

Turn a finding, or any idea, into a tracked change: fewer deliveries from 1 June, measured against March to May, with groceries watched alongside. Each review compares the baseline with what has happened since and says which dates it used. One quiet week is called thin evidence. The app describes the change; it does not claim the intention caused it.

Intentions can be edited, paused, resumed, and completed without touching any transaction.

## Inbox

New findings, open questions from the importer or interpreter, failed imports, failed exports, and failed scans arrive in the inbox, each linking to where it can be dealt with. Nothing appears twice for the same unchanged thing.

## Scans and reminders

Opening Insights runs a scan of current data. A nightly scan does the same. A failed scan is visible and can be rerun. An optional reminder says the data is getting stale when nothing has been imported for a while; it does not claim anything changed.

## Example

A streaming subscription rises from $15.99 to $18.99 and a finding appears with the two amounts and the month it changed. The user dismisses it. Four months later it rises again and a new finding appears. Separately, the user tracks "fewer deliveries" from June; the July review shows delivery down $180 a month against the baseline and groceries up $60, with one month noted as early days.
