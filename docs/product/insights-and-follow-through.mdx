## Delivery scope

All behavior on this page belongs to [stage 7](/stages/follow-through). It adds findings, intentions, the general inbox, reminders, and scheduled follow-up to the existing financial and analyst experiences. Earlier stages already show jobs and failures in their owning views.

The Insights view presents findings worth investigating, including gradual cost increases, changed recurring payments, unusual fees, and shifts in purchase frequency. A finding needs evidence and relevance to the user's history.

## A useful finding

Each finding states what changed, the comparison period, the amount involved, and the supporting records. It distinguishes a confirmed change from a possible explanation.

A subscription increase can show the old and new amounts. Repeated fees can show their cumulative cost. An unusual transaction can show why it differs from comparable history. The analyst can investigate potentially overlapping services and costs that tend to occur together. Those explanations cite the supporting schedules or transactions and distinguish association from cause. Payments alone do not prove service usage or whether a subscription is unnecessary.

Findings are ranked by material amount and persistence. Several observations about the same underlying change are grouped. Tiny changes with large percentages do not automatically outrank substantial costs.

## Review flow

The user can inspect the evidence, ask the analyst to investigate, mark a finding useful, dismiss it, or add context. A dismissed finding stays dismissed. A new change period can create a new finding with its supporting evidence.

Adding an explanation can change the interpretation. For example, a visiting family member can explain a temporary increase. The app remembers the relevant period without applying the explanation to unrelated future spending.

Corrected or newly imported records can invalidate a finding. The app marks it superseded rather than continuing to present it as current.

## Follow a deliberate change

The user can turn a finding into a tracked intention, such as reducing delivery orders while protecting social dining. The intention records the chosen measure, comparison period, start date, and relevant context. The user can edit, pause, or complete it without changing the underlying transactions.

Later reviews compare actual outcomes with that baseline. They include related changes, such as higher grocery spending, when the user has chosen to track the combined effect.

The app distinguishes progress from insufficient evidence. A single quiet week does not prove a lasting habit change. It does not assume that a correlation proves the intention caused the outcome.

## Notification behavior

Opening Insights runs a bounded scan of current records. Scheduled scans can run the same operation while the user is offline. A failed scan can be retried from the owning screen.

The default notification channel is the in-app inbox. It lists new findings, failures and questions requiring an answer. Unchanged findings do not produce repeated alerts.

Scheduled reviews can revisit confirmed commitments or tracked intentions. With no new transaction data, the app can remind the user to import records but cannot claim a new spending observation.
