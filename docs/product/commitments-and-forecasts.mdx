All behavior here belongs to [stage 5](/stages/plan-costs). The stage includes the complete planning UI, saved assumptions, and deterministic calculations. Future estimates remain distinct from actual bank transactions.

## Recurring payments

The app suggests recurring payments from history and shows the supporting dates and amounts. The user confirms the amount, cadence, payment account, next date, and effective period before a schedule enters the baseline outlook. Mortgage payments, strata, insurance, and subscriptions can each have their own schedule.

A quarterly levy remains quarterly in the cash calendar. Its ordinary-cost contribution can be spread across its service period. Changes preserve earlier effective amounts and accounts. The user can inspect or correct a payment's occurrence match. A missing payment alone does not cancel a schedule.

## Historical measures

Income minus personal costs is historical surplus. Cash changes come from evidenced deposit balances. Loan repayment effects distinguish repayments, separately dated interest and fees, borrowing, and other adjustments. These remain separate measures rather than a combined savings score or claim of complete net worth.

## Cost outlook

Choose 30 or 90 days. Inspect recent covered weeks, estimated variable spending, confirmed commitments, expected income, and explicit one-offs. Each included scheduled cost appears once, with its historical payments removed from the variable baseline.

Show the baseline dates, actual week count, exclusions, and assumptions. If no covered baseline exists, the overall estimate is unavailable while the separate commitment calendar still works. The app never fills missing bank history with invented transactions.

The outlook shows estimated costs and surplus under those assumptions. It does not present probability ranges or claim a statistically calibrated prediction.

## Cash calendar

List known scheduled deposit movements, including card settlements and loan repayments. Those movements are distinct from purchase costs and interest. The same card purchase is not counted again as spending when it is repaid.

Show the latest evidenced account balances with their dates as historical context. This stage does not predict future cash or debt balances. An estimated cost reduction is not labelled money available in a bank account. Users can enter known future payment amounts and dates without configuring interest or card-cycle simulation.

## Scenarios

Copy the baseline assumptions and change a category amount, commitment amount, or one-off. Show the estimated cost, income, or credit difference over the same horizon. A reduction in delivery can include an explicit increase in groceries; the app does not invent that substitution.

Save the assumptions for reuse. Opening or refreshing the scenario calculates from current records using the selected baseline period. Neither the scenario nor its calculation alters historical transactions or confirmed commitments.

[Calculation rules](/technical/calculation-rules#forecast-contract) defines the arithmetic and its limits.
