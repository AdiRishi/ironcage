The Commitments view groups recurring expenses and expected income. The Forecasts view combines those schedules with variable spending and explicit assumptions. Future values are visibly distinct from actual transactions.

## Recurring payments

The app detects likely recurring services from payment history. Each entry shows the merchant, amount or expected range, cadence, last payment, and estimated next date. It distinguishes a proposed pattern from a confirmed commitment. Proposed schedules affect a forecast only when the user explicitly includes them as assumptions.

The user can correct the next date, amount, cadence, payment account, service period, or status. Mortgage payments, strata, insurance, and subscriptions can each have their own schedule. A quarterly levy remains quarterly in the cash calendar. Amount or schedule changes take effect on a recorded date and preserve earlier schedule segments. The user can inspect, confirm, or remove the match between a payment and its scheduled occurrence.

A changed amount prompts a comparison with prior payments. A missing payment is a possible lapse or missing record, not proof of cancellation. A subscription payment alone does not reveal whether the service was used.

Each view explains its included accounts and period. The app does not combine these measures into an unexplained savings score. It does not claim complete net worth from bank records alone.

## Forecast flow

The user chooses a 30-, 90-, or 365-day horizon and opens the assumptions behind the forecast. The app shows expected income, known commitments, estimated variable spending, and a range of possible cash balances.

Cash forecasts start from a reconciled balance date. Without a reliable balance anchor, the affected account has no predicted cash balance. Known scheduled payments remain visible. If records stop before today, the intervening period is also an estimate. A stale balance is never presented as a live bank balance.

The user can inspect and confirm how variable purchases are divided between payment accounts and which personal events leave the forecast training history. Those exclusions are saved forecast assumptions, separate from ordinary-cost exclusions. Variable bills use their expected amount in each forecast path; their displayed amount ranges are not additional simulated uncertainty. The cash forecast shows gross charges and assumed incoming credits separately. Fixed monthly loan interest and fee assumptions are shown separately from repayments; changes in rates or offset balances are not automatically projected.

The app lists known large upcoming expenses separately from ordinary variable spending. Forecasts avoid counting both card purchases and their settlements as additional personal spending, while still representing when cash leaves the deposit account.

## Scenarios

A scenario changes named assumptions without changing historical records. The user can compare fewer delivery orders, a different subscription cost, or a planned purchase with the current baseline.

Each comparison shows the expected effect, the horizon, and unchanged assumptions. A missing cash anchor leaves the cash delta unavailable while supported cost effects remain visible. A reduction in one category can include an explicit increase elsewhere. The app does not assume every reduction translates into equal savings.

Scenarios can be saved and later compared with actual outcomes. A saved forecast distinguishes a fixed start date from a start date that resolves to today when refreshed. Scenario assumptions remain editable and distinguishable from confirmed facts.

## Uncertainty and missing information

Forecast ranges reflect historical variation and the assumptions used. A range without enough evaluation history is labelled uncalibrated. The annual calibration gate requires about 20 years of non-overlapping target observations plus training history, so annual ranges normally remain uncalibrated in a personal dataset. When the records cannot support a range, the app displays the known schedules and names the missing assumptions. Wider horizons can have greater uncertainty. Sparse or incomplete history produces a limited forecast with an explanation, not an invented precise answer.

The app measures forecast performance against later observations and makes forecast error available. It can report that a simpler method performed better and use that method for subsequent forecasts.
