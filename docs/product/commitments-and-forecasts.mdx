title: Commitments and forecasts
description: Understand recurring costs, see likely future cash flow, and compare deliberate changes.

Commitments groups recurring expenses and expected income. Forecasts combines those schedules with variable spending and explicit assumptions. Future values are visibly distinct from actual transactions.

## Recurring payments

The app identifies likely recurring services from payment history. Each entry shows the merchant, amount or expected range, cadence, last payment, and estimated next date. It distinguishes an inferred pattern from a confirmed commitment.

Adi can correct the next date, amount, cadence, or status. Mortgage payments, strata, insurance, and subscriptions can each have their own schedule. A quarterly levy remains quarterly in the cash calendar.

A changed amount prompts a comparison with prior payments. A missing payment is a possible lapse or missing record, not proof of cancellation. A subscription payment alone does not reveal whether the service was used.

## Several views of progress

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Money left after costs">
    Income minus personal costs shows the surplus generated during a period. Loan principal
    repayment and transfers between owned accounts are separate from those costs.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cash balances">
    Changes in deposit balances show where available cash increased or decreased. Moving cash
    between two included accounts does not increase the combined balance.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Debt reduction">
    Principal payments show how much debt was reduced. New borrowing, interest, and fees remain
    separately identifiable.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Each view explains its included accounts and period. The app does not combine these measures into an unexplained savings score. It does not claim complete net worth from bank records alone.

## Forecast flow

Adi chooses a horizon and opens the assumptions behind the forecast. The app shows expected income, known commitments, estimated variable spending, and a range of possible cash balances.

The forecast starts from a reconciled balance date. If records stop before today, the intervening period is also an estimate. A stale balance is never presented as a live bank balance.

The app identifies known large upcoming expenses separately from ordinary variable spending. Forecasts avoid counting both card purchases and their settlements as additional personal spending, while still representing when cash leaves the deposit account.

## Scenarios

A scenario changes named assumptions without changing historical records. Adi can compare fewer delivery orders, a different subscription cost, or a planned purchase with the current baseline.

Each comparison shows the expected effect, the horizon, and unchanged assumptions. A reduction in one category can include an explicit increase elsewhere. The app does not assume every reduction translates into equal savings.

Scenarios can be saved and later compared with actual outcomes. Scenario assumptions remain editable and distinguishable from confirmed facts.

## Uncertainty and missing information

Forecast ranges reflect historical variation and the assumptions used. Wider horizons can have greater uncertainty. Sparse or incomplete history produces a limited forecast with an explanation, not an invented precise answer.

The app measures forecast performance against later observations and makes forecast error available. It can report that a simpler method performed better and use that method for subsequent forecasts.
