---
title: Questions
description: What Ironcage needs you to confirm, ordered by how much money it affects.
---

Questions collect everything that needs a decision from you, largest amount first. Each one says what Ironcage thinks, why, and what it affects. You answer with one selection.

## Kinds of question

| Question             | Example                                                                                             |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who is this?         | `SQ *BLUE BOTTLE 0412` could be a café or a market stall. Proposed: Blue Bottle Coffee, café.       |
| What is this person? | Twelve monthly payments of $1,840 to Jane Smith. Proposed: rent.                                    |
| Is this yours?       | $5,000 to an account ending 9921 that is not one of the four. Proposed: your own account elsewhere. |
| Which category?      | A counterparty the model could not place confidently.                                               |
| Which account?       | A CSV file that does not name its account.                                                          |
| Which row is which?  | Two same-day, same-amount rows the importer cannot pair on its own.                                 |

A question about a counterparty is asked once, not once per transaction. Answering it applies to every transaction from that counterparty that you have not set by hand.

## Answering

Accept applies the proposal. Choosing something else records your answer as a correction and teaches the counterparty. Skip leaves it open. The overview shows how much money open questions affect in the selected period.

Source reviews from the importer, such as which account a CSV belongs to or which row matches which, appear in the same list and resolve the same way.
