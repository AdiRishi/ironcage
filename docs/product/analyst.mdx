The analyst is on the [roadmap](/roadmap). This page describes what it will do.

## Asking

Ask from a blank prompt, or from anything selected: a category, a counterparty, a transaction, a stream in the flow. The selection is attached to the question.

> Why was August more expensive than July?

> How much did the Japan trip cost, including the flights booked in March?

> What subscriptions do I pay for, and which ones started this year?

## Answers

The analyst answers by calling the same read operations the screens use. It states the period, accounts, and coverage it used, and links every figure to its records. It never computes a total itself. When the data cannot answer, it says what is missing.

## Proposed changes

The analyst can propose a change, such as marking a dinner as a work expense. The proposal appears as a card with Accept and Ignore. Nothing changes until you accept, and acceptance goes through the same checks as any other edit. Text in a bank description is data, never an instruction.

## Briefings

At the start of each month, the analyst writes a short briefing of the previous month from the overview's computed facts: what came in, what went out, what changed, and what needs an answer.
