Ironcage is a personal wealth operating system: one engine running many investment strategies, each constrained by deterministic risk rules it cannot override, each earning its capital through evidence. AI informs decisions and designs improvements; deterministic code enforces limits; evidence decides what scales.

This page is the front door to the specification. Three rules govern the whole set:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The documents form a hierarchy">
    [Vision](/vision) says why the system exists and states its principles. The [product
    chapters](/product) say what it does, observably. The [technical chapters](/technical) say how
    it is built. A lower document that weakens a higher one is wrong and gets fixed — deliberately,
    with the change recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chapters are the authority for the current design">
    The [decision appendix](/technical/appendix/decisions) is an archive of how choices were made
    and unmade. Read it for history or to avoid re-arguing a settled question — never to find out
    what the system does. The [ADRs](/adr) sit outside this hierarchy and describe the repository
    rather than the system: file layout, build and test wiring, tooling conventions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unsettled things are visible">
    Proposed values are tagged in each chapter's values table; open questions sit in each chapter's
    Open questions section with their safe fallbacks. Nothing undecided hides inside a confident
    adjective.
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Style](/style) is the writing contract every page here follows.
