# Ironcage

Ironcage is a personal wealth operating system: one engine running many investment strategies, every one caged by deterministic risk rules, every one earning its capital through evidence.

The ubiquitous language of the domain lives in [`docs/glossary.mdx`](./docs/glossary.mdx) — the naming rules, every term, and the words to avoid beside each one. Read it before naming anything. On the docs site (`pnpm docs:dev`) it renders at `/glossary`.

The vision lives at [`docs/vision.mdx`](./docs/vision.mdx) and the specification under [`docs/product/`](./docs/product/) and [`docs/technical/`](./docs/technical/).
